# Evidence notes

Read the sections relevant to the candidate. These distinctions matter more than a long checklist.

## An evidence record

For a material claim retain: exact chain + contract; pool or recipient if relevant; actual source URL; original publication/event time when known; observation time; unit/window/denominator; what was observed; and what remains unverified. A retrieval timestamp is not a provider's market timestamp. If the provider gives only rolling windows, say so.

A project's docs can establish a described mechanism. Configuration can establish the configured recipient and control. A transaction can establish a particular execution. Neither alone establishes adoption, recurring revenue, all-time payments, or future execution.

## Identity and competing contracts

Compare the full addresses, quote assets, metadata, linked originals, and actual mechanics. Shared links establish reuse. Even an exact website link can be copied. Seek a primary statement or execution binding a thesis-critical affiliation or benefit to the candidate. Keep independently discovered project origins separate from the candidate's self-listed domain. No match in a catalog is not a rejection of an early project.

A quote pair with WBTC is not evidence that holders receive BTC. A fee-to-social-account configuration is not an endorsement by that person. Collected creator fees are distinct from explicit adoption of that mint. Preserve attribution when the original post is unreadable.

## Pool state and price

Check whether the token is the base or quote asset: a provider's `priceUsd` may price the base only. Do not borrow a different pool's identity or units to fill gaps. Preserve both observations when providers disagree.

USD liquidity can fall because asset prices changed. Token reserve changes, position events, and USD totals are distinct. Position liquidity units at different price ranges cannot be added as comparable dollar shares. A position withdrawal doesn't establish who beneficially owns it or why they withdrew.

Virtual pricing reserves are distinct from deposited quote assets available for exits. For exact PumpSwap observations using that protocol's supplied accounting fields, effective quote pricing reserve is quote vault plus signed virtual quote; real quote reserve is quote vault less pending protocol and creator fees. Do not apply that formula to other AMMs or subtract pending fees twice. A reserve ratio isn't a realizable USD exit quote.

Tiny integer-denominated transfers can produce extreme last-trade ratios due to rounding and fees. Inspect raw amounts, token decimals, any UI scaling, source clocks, and the pool reserve basis before calling a breakout. A sampled transaction is not a complete trade history or proof of manipulation.

## Activity and holders

Hourly USD volume and hourly buy/sell transaction counts describe that hour. Their quotient is an aggregate average, not a distribution of trade sizes or a count of independent traders. Daily volume cannot substitute for a missing hourly amount.

Keep infrastructure balances out of unsupported claims about investor concentration. Identify Solana token-account owners and vault authorities when possible. Contract ownership or a familiar label alone may be insufficient. Pool vault balances can grow as people sell.

An earliest indexed EVM mint-block allocation is not necessarily pool opening or today's distribution. Net end-of-block balances avoid counting the same tokens repeatedly. Keep the original minted-supply denominator. Distinct recipients aren't verified independent buyers. Bounded external funding samples omit other funding paths; an exchange, bridge, relayer, or launch service is a plausible common funder.

## Security and control

Keep contract safety distinct from market integrity and economic merit. A negative automated checker is not a security audit. Conflicting positive findings require their mechanisms and coverage to be explained rather than averaged away.

On Solana, inspect actual mint extensions and authorities. Token-2022 membership doesn't establish a reward extension; an external fee program is not a mint extension. Distinguish mutable transfer fees, withdrawal authority, mint/freeze permissions, and unrelated creator payments.

On EVM, public owner renunciation doesn't rule out other balance or allowance control paths. A read-only simulation can establish observed behavior at that block, including an authorization concern; it doesn't prove funds were actually stolen. Failed coverage remains unknown. Avoid sending transactions or requesting wallet credentials for research.

## Reviewing an earlier call

Freeze the evidence cutoff. Separate the originally available facts from later observations. Review the positive case, its strongest alternative, the non-selection option, and all outcomes. A later winner cannot prove earlier identity or execution claims; a later loss cannot prove its predictability.

When a thesis-critical claim was overstated, correct the full current conclusion and identify the concrete source. A newly noticed old role, website, cumulative payment, or team post is not automatically a new catalyst.
